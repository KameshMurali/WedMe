# Marketing media

Owned assets for the marketing site. Generated with Higgsfield and committed, so
nothing here is a third-party dependency at runtime — the same reason the
ceremony photographs and the destination hero shots are committed rather than
hotlinked.

| file | where | why |
|---|---|---|
| `breather-girih.webp` | the seam before the FAQ (`page.tsx`) | A night courtyard under strung lanterns. |
| `breather-chapel.webp` | the seam before the closing CTA | A garden path at blue hour, after the ceremony. |

Both are 1760x754 (roughly 21:9) and deliberately DARK: they sit in the ink run
at the bottom of the homepage, and `.breather-scrim` fades each band into the
page ground at top and bottom so it ends in the page rather than against it. A
bright image here would punch a hole in the ground and the gold ornament drawn
over it would stop reading.

Replacing one: keep it dark, keep the detail away from the horizontal centre
where the ornament sits, and keep it under ~100KB — these are the last two
images on a 12,000px page.
